Audio Labeler

Everything runs in this browser. No upload, no install, no network. Your answers are saved locally as you go.

1  Folder

Subfolders are included. WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC and M4A are read; anything else is ignored.

2  Categories

Keys 1–9 map to these in order. Asking what kind of sound is often worth more than yes/no: knowing a false positive is thunder rather than merely "not the target" tells you which negatives to mine.

3  Options

Shuffling matters when clips come from the same recording: neighbours arrive adjacent, and labelling a run of them invites a run of identical answers. Hide filenames if they carry model scores — a listener who knows the model was confident labels differently.
